Get started

Quick check

  1. Type a website at the top (example: mybusiness.co.za).
  2. Press Check and wait for it to finish.
  3. Click the row to open Problems & fixes.
  4. Press ← All websites to go back to the full list.

Many customers at once

  1. Use an Excel/CSV with columns Client Website and URL.
  2. Click Import list and choose the file.
  3. Open a site and press Check again.

What the colours mean

  • Looks good — no action needed.
  • Needs attention — fix soon.
  • Something's wrong — likely breaking site or email.
  • Couldn't check — your network failed the test; try again before escalating.

Useful buttons

  • Download report — ZIP to your PC (HTML, CSV, JSON).
  • i on a column — short plain-English explanation.
  • Settings — wait time, auto checks, alert webhooks.
